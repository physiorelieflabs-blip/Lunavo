---
name: Referrer subscription discount
description: Verified referral rewards belong to the merchant who shared the code and grant a fixed 30 USD subscription discount.
---

The qualifying referred merchant receives no referral discount. After the referred merchant completes one verified subscription payment, the referrer earns one non-withdrawable fixed 30 USD-equivalent discount for the referrer's next subscription cycle, converted using that cycle's locked currency rate.

**Why:** The user explicitly wants the person who refers to receive the benefit, and a fixed USD-equivalent amount is stable across subscription currencies.

**How to apply:** Keep rewards attached to the referrer merchant, store the converted amount in the referrer's subscription currency, consume it only on that referrer's next eligible cycle, and reverse it if the qualifying payment is reversed.
