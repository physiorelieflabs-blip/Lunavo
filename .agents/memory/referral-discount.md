---
name: Referrer subscription discount
description: Verified referral rewards belong to the merchant who shared the code and grant a 30 percent subscription discount.
---

The qualifying referred merchant receives no referral discount. After the referred merchant completes one verified subscription payment, the referrer earns one non-withdrawable 30 percent discount for the referrer's next subscription cycle, calculated from that cycle's gross amount in its locked currency.

**Why:** The user explicitly confirmed the referrer benefit is 30 percent, so a $30 subscription produces a $9 discount while other billing currencies use the same percentage of their locked gross amount.

**How to apply:** Keep rewards attached to the referrer merchant, calculate and store the discount in the referrer's subscription currency, consume it only on that referrer's next eligible cycle, and reverse it if the qualifying payment is reversed.

The first 150 verified, non-reversed referral rewards also grant one idempotent 12-month subscription-only entitlement. It is not cash and does not create a second reward path.
