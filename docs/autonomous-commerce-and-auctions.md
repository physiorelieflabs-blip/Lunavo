# Lunavo Autonomous Commerce & Auctions

## Auction sections

Lunavo has two distinct auction areas:

1. **Product Auctions** — physical goods, vehicles, jewelry, watches, electronics, art, collectibles, fashion, sneakers, furniture, antiques, rare books, coins, memorabilia, musical instruments, handmade/limited-edition goods, agricultural/industrial equipment, tickets and other eligible products.
2. **Store Auctions** — acquisition of an entire Lunavo merchant store as a business asset.

A merchant store may enter a store auction only when Lunavo's authoritative financial ledger verifies at least **$500,000 USD-equivalent in cumulative profit**. The master admin is exempt for stores created by the admin AI. Eligibility must never rely on a merchant-entered profit figure.

## Store-auction disclosure

Before bidding, buyers receive verified store performance information including revenue/profit history, orders, customers, average order value, growth, traffic, conversion, advertising performance, expenses, inventory, product performance, store age and relevant risks. Sales/revenue history is visualised as graphs. Financial metrics come from Lunavo's authoritative records.

A successful store acquisition performs an atomic ownership transfer. Owner-level access is revoked from the former owner and granted to the buyer, with an immutable ownership-transfer audit record. Assets transferred must respect privacy, legal, payment, domain and subscription boundaries.

## Autonomous stores

Every Lunavo store can use an autonomous AI operator. The AI continuously researches products, prioritises **high demand + low competition**, evaluates margin, supplier reliability, growth and risk, creates/optimises listings, monitors inventory and performance, produces marketing content, and improves the catalogue using actual results.

The AI must never fabricate supplier availability, sales, profit, advertising activity, balances or market evidence.

## AI pricing

The AI calculates a commercially sensible recommended price and maximum viable price from authoritative supplier/landed cost, market range, fees, shipping, demand, competition and target margin. It must not invent a price when required source data is unavailable.

## AI-created admin stores

The master admin has an **AI Store Creator**. The admin may optionally enter a natural-language description such as `I want a medical product store`. The AI determines the store concept, researches the market, identifies high-demand/low-competition opportunities, finds suitable suppliers and supported integrations, creates the storefront, catalogue, descriptions, SEO and marketing assets, and continuously operates the store.

The AI should discover the external connectors required for the requested workflow. It may only use connectors that are actually available and authorised. It must request required authorisation instead of pretending an account is connected.

Admin-created stores are exempt from the merchant store-auction profit threshold.

## Daily AI advertising

Every store can have autonomous daily advertising. The admin can configure the number of ads/posts to be produced per day. The AI distributes the configured quantity over the day, selects products based on performance/opportunity, creates varied creative/copy, publishes only through actually connected/authorised channels, and reports results. The admin can change the quantity or pause automation.

## Security and accounting boundaries

Automation never bypasses payment verification, ledger controls, inventory reservation semantics, tenant authorisation, connector authentication, privacy restrictions or audit logging. External providers remain adapters; Lunavo's internal transaction and ownership records remain authoritative where specified by the platform architecture.
